## WINNING COPY PATTERNS

What already works in this account, and what buyers type:

1. **Hire a Filipino / Philippines VA** — said like a person, not “PH VA”
2. **You interview. You pick.** — matches the real process
3. **Dedicated teammate, not a gig marketplace**
4. **Agency / firm / company / staffing** — those are the words people search
5. **Tell us who you need** — already on the live pages
6. Full sentences in descriptions. Contractions. No keyword stuffed into every headline

Competitor patterns I used, not copied:

- MyOutDesk: build the team, cut overhead, book a conversation, industry-specific seats. I did **not** use 70%, 0.7%, 8,500 clients, or hire-in-7-days
- Wing: dedicated person owns the work, not a marketplace, inbox / CRM / follow-up. I did **not** use 48 hours or top 1%
- Magic: agency interviews and trains. Their model is more concierge. We stay a staffing company
