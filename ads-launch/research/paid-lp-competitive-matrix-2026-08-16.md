Read-only inspection. Tracking params stripped. Viewports **1440×900** and **390×844**. First safe funnel steps only — no forms submitted, no chat, no scheduler, no PII entered.

Screenshots: `ads-launch/research/screenshots/competitors/`.

## Recurring mechanics vs visual fashion

**Mechanics (show up across unlike designs):**

1. **Tangible outcome in the first viewport** — shortlist, seats, instant price, a week back, a quote — not “strategy call.”
2. **Progressive commitment** — a non-contact choice first (Outsourcey, AbroadWorks, VirtualStaff CTA→signup, Emapta one email) **or** a full form paired with a lower-commitment sidestep (OutsourcingSuccess browse profiles, Virtustant phone/email/calendar).
3. **Immediate proof** — stars/review count *before or beside* the ask (Virtustant, Outsourcey, OutsourcingSuccess).
4. **Price or process transparency** — even if the number is a platform fee, an hourly, or a 48-hour clock.
5. **People as proof, not decoration** — small real-looking faces, office/network of humans, or a named VA photo *supporting* the offer (not a third competing hero column).
6. **Multiple ways through** — call, calendar, browse, “I know what I want,” email.

**Fashion (do not copy):** orange SaaS, teal network maps, pineapple fruit, LATAM vs PH, “top 1%,” cookie walls, chat popups, dark navy AI-era heroes.

Do not pick a page to clone. The shared conversion idea is: **make the first click feel like progress on hiring, not like giving a salesperson your phone number.**

## Current VC (live `/us`, for contrast — not a competitor)

Form already in the hero. Visible PII: name, work email, phone, optional company website. CTA: **Book a Free Strategy Call**. Trust chips (LinkedIn/FB reach, Google, Clutch, years) sit in the hero; named testimonials sit **below**. Desktop at ≥1160px is three competing columns: copy · large portrait · form. Phone is secondary (“Prefer to talk?”). Outcome described is a consultation.
