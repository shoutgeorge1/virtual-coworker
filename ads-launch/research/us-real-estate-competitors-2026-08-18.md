# US real-estate VA competitor research — 18 Aug 2026

Checked from public pages, comparison articles, and search snippets. Google Ads Transparency Center HTML is a signed-in shell and did not return ad copy in this pass. Paid ads were not clicked.

## What advertisers emphasize (not assumed)

Recurring employer-facing themes across current public pages:

1. **Lead follow-up / CRM / database** — the most consistent commercial promise (MyOutDesk, Data Qualytic, VA Masters, GigaBPO listicles).
2. **Listing admin and transaction paperwork** — calendars, docs, signatures, MLS/listing updates. Specialists sell “transaction coordinator”; general staffing firms sell admin around files.
3. **Marketing support** — portals, social, listing copy. Present, not the only offer.
4. **Appointment setting / ISA / cold calling** — prominent at MyOutDesk, REVA, VA4REI. Real, but not the only demand cluster. Do not make it the dominant Virtual Coworker promise.
5. **Property-management admin** — tenant communication, lease files, maintenance inbox, owner updates (Wing, Vitalis, Office Beacon, VA Staff). Software names (AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi) are common. Virtual Coworker has not confirmed AppFolio-experienced recruiting.

Audience split: agents/teams/brokerages (MyOutDesk, Virtudesk, WVS) vs investors/wholesalers (REVA, VA4REI) vs property managers (Wing, Vitalis). Virtual Coworker’s one page covers all three without three URLs.
