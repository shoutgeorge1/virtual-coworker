Your Questions Answered: Hiring Virtual Assistants & Remote Staff

From startups to established businesses, get clear answers on how our dedicated Filipino virtual assistants and remote staff provide high-quality, cost-effective solutions to help you scale efficiently.

The Philippines is a global hub for virtual assistants due to its highly educated and English-proficient workforce. With a 94% literacy rate and ranking as the world’s fourth-largest English-speaking country, you gain access to skilled professionals – many with degrees in business, tech, and communications – ready to seamlessly integrate into your operations.

Hiring offshore staff involves significant work. Virtual Coworker streamlines this by handling all pre-screening, reference checks, payroll, and HR requirements for you. This saves you a huge amount of time and effort, allowing you to focus on your core business instead of recruitment complexities.

For your convenience, we accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express with a minimal surcharge. We also offer secure and straightforward ACH-debit payments.

Transparency is key. All our virtual staff are required to use our proprietary time-logging software. This system monitors activity by taking desktop screenshots every 10 minutes and generates daily activity reports for your review, ensuring you only pay for verified, productive work hours
