How these numbers are calculated (Blended attribution methodology)
  • Blended Attribution: Google Ads represents our primary marketing spend. Unit costs (e.g. Cost per Enquiry, Cost per Discovery Call) divide total Google Ads investment by sales-confirmed employer outcomes in the period. While individual leads may not each carry a direct 1:1 click-ID attribution yet, blended attribution provides leadership with dependable directional insight into acquisition efficiency.
  • Sales-Confirmed Outcomes: Sourced directly from Cheyenne Gichana (US) and Holly Wallace (AU) weekly reviews. Excludes non-employer contacts (job seekers, spam, and vendor solicitations).
  • Lagging Funnel Dynamics: Discovery calls, job orders, and placements naturally mature 1–4 weeks after initial enquiries. Reported numbers reflect operational volume in the period rather than closed cohorts.
  • Direct Click Attribution Progress: 1:1 click-ID capture (GCLID) is being instrumented across all website forms to pass directly into Zoho CRM, enabling granular channel ROAS reporting alongside blended performance.
