How these numbers are calculated
  • Blended Cost per Enquiry: Google Ads spend divided by all sales-confirmed employer enquiries. This is an operational efficiency metric, not paid Google Ads CPA.
  • Sales-Confirmed Outcomes: Sourced from Cheyenne Gichana (US) and Holly Wallace (AU) weekly reviews. Excludes job seekers, spam, solicitors, and invalid leads.
  • Lagging Funnel: Job orders and placements take 2–4 weeks from initial discovery calls. Current month numbers reflect activity in period, not a closed cohort.
  • Ad Attribution: Only leads containing a verified advertising click ID (GCLID) are counted as directly attributed. Coverage is currently 5 of 31 reviewed records.
