Section 4

CRM / lead routing clues

  • Buyer leads: Gravity Forms POST back to WordPress. Destination unknown without submitting.
  • Ops system: admin.virtualcoworker.com is a custom Rails app — fulfillment / portal likely outside WP.
  • Talent leads: PH site routes to Zoho Recruit — separate from buyer forms.
  • Support: Zendesk can become a second sales-ish inbox if not routed cleanly.

Cannot confirm form → CRM is 1:1, duplicated, or manual. Discovery item for a paid engagement.

Section 5

Buyer vs candidate funnel contamination

Critical Consultation form includes a job-seeker option. On /contact-us/, fields include staffing hours and “I am searching for a job” next to buyer fields — mixes candidate traffic into the paid consult path. Proof: live contact form · Findings write-up

High US buyer chrome constantly points to PH careers. Footer CTA → virtualcoworker.com.ph. Good intent, but candidate traffic is one click from every buyer page — and tracking stacks diverge.

High PH site still exposes buyer-ish URLs (pricing, services, link back to US/AU) while primary job is careers. Cross-links without hard funnel walls = analytics and SEO bleed.

Section 9

Client discovery questions

Framed for a paid engagement — not role/interview questions.

1. Who owns GTM / GA4 / Google Ads / Meta — internal, freelancer, or agency — and is there one source of truth? Explains hardcoded Meta vs GTM Google tags, and US vs AU container drift.
2. Where do Gravity Forms submissions land, and is “I am searching for a job” still supposed to be on the consult form? Highest-ROI cleanup; also maps lead routing for offline conversion work.
3. How should buyer (.com / .com.au) vs talent (.ph + Zoho) analytics be separated? Prevents candidate traffic from contaminating paid buyer conversion reporting.
4. Which conversion is the money event — and which fire in GTM / Ads / Meta today? GTM mentions generate_lead / purchase / Schedule; match to real sales stages.
5. Should Zendesk chat create sales leads, support tickets, or both? Second front door; often invisible in ROAS math.

Executive

Top findings

  1. US stack is mature-ish (WP + GTM + GA4 + Ads + Meta + Zendesk) but Meta is partially outside GTM.
  2. AU and US run different GTM/GA4/Ads IDs — regional silos.
  3. PH careers site is a separate funnel (Zoho Recruit) with basically no marketing tag parity.
  4. Buyer consult form explicitly accepts job seekers — clearest public hygiene failure.
  5. Custom Rails admin suggests real ops backend; form→CRM glue is the next paid-audit map.
