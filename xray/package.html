Discovery questions

The things worth asking Virtual Coworker directly, because they cannot be answered from outside. The CRM and lead-routing ones are also listed on Lead Flow & Zoho.

Must ask

Where do ads land?

  • Meta ads — Instant Form, landing page, or main site?
  • Google ads — which URLs? Main WP, offsite LPs, or something else?
  • Source of truth — which form / CRM wins when leads come from chat, phone, and forms?

Public site looks form-heavy. We can’t see Instant Forms or offsite LPs from HTML alone.

Forms & CRM

Where do submits go?

  • Email inbox, CRM, Zapier/Make, or nowhere visible?
  • Is “I am searching for a job” on the hire form intentional?
  • Same pipeline for US form 9 / AU form 8 / Meta LP form 12?

Ownership

Who owns ads / GTM / Meta?

  • US Ads vs AU Ads — same person or agency?
  • Who publishes GTM vs who edits the WordPress theme?
  • Who owns the Meta pixel in the theme?

Money

What’s the conversion that matters?

  • Raw form submit, booked consult, or signed client?
  • Which of those fire in Google Ads / Meta / GA4 today?
  • Does Zendesk chat count as a lead?

Markets

Geo + PH + brand feel

  • Soft PH banner forever, or hard block + VPN handling?
  • Should PH reverse banner (push US visitors to hire) be live?
  • Is PH inbound big enough to charge for job alerts?
  • Should AU feel Australian vs same skin as US?

Direction

Purpose-built pages for ad traffic

  • Would they support a small paid layer running beside the existing sites?
  • Is a subdomain on their own domain available for those pages?
