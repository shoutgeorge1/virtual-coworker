## Do not block globally

| Term | Why |
|------|-----|
| hire | Strong employer intent |
| hiring | Strong employer intent |

Add `hire` / `hiring` as negatives only in a **job-seeker** campaign (none in Phase 1), never as account-wide blocks on employer campaigns.

## Match-type note

Prefer exact (and later phrase) positives. Negatives can be broad enough to catch variants, then tighten from search-term reports after launch.
