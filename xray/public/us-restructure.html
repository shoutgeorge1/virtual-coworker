What buyers call this

Search-term insights

Two different markets, two different dictionaries:

  • History (ST cost share): virtual assistant 50% · Philippines 24% · hire 7% · VA shorthand 5% · Filipino 3% · agency 2% · staffing / remote staffing under 1%.
  • Pilot clicks (4–13 Aug): the terms that actually got clicked were generic VA / hire, then remote staffing agency, then job-seeker junk (now mostly excluded), then bookkeeping. Exact Philippines heads are starved (CORE lost-IS-rank 38%).
  • A buyer typing remote staffing agency is closer to Virtual Coworker than a buyer typing bare virtual assistant. The current CORE fights that by dumping staffing queries into Hire_VA_PH RSAs.
  • Intent: A = remote staffing agency, cs outsource, filipino staffing agency, VA agency philippines. E = va workers ph (47c / $93, 24% CTR, excluded), virtual assistant jobs, careers. D = WFH appointment setter, remote CSR, administrative assistant remote.

Human marketing, not Ad Strength

RSA insights

Headline asset CTR, 4–13 Aug (overlapping — one impression can show several lines):

  • Place + staffing + company wins: Virtual Staff Philippines 28.4% · Philippines VA Company 23.3% · US Hours Remote Staff 21.3% · Offshore Staffing Agency (KW) 19.3% · Filipino Virtual Assistant 18.1% · Hire Filipino VA 13–15%.
  • Generic VA services 12.1%. Role-title headlines 10.6%. “Employer Hiring Only” 8.4%. “Not a Job Board VA” 6.9% — defensive copy does not make a business owner click, and it is not a proven junk filter.
  • Concepts that are working: Filipino, Philippines, staffing, agency, remote staff, dedicated, hire, US hours. Recruit / vet / shortlist is under-shown (107 impr) but not a loser. Cost / savings was not a live headline theme.
  • Appointment Setter KW 23.3% CTR — same trap as the setter AG. Do not scale it.
