Conversion actions

Needs access

The first thing I check. Duplicate, stale, or overly generous conversion actions quietly distort every decision the account makes.

  • Which actions are set to primary
  • Whether one lead can count more than once
  • Attribution window and model
Requires Google Ads access. Pairs with GA4 access to confirm both sides tell the same story.

Search terms & waste

Needs access

Usually the fastest saving available in any employer-side account: paid clicks from people looking for a job rather than looking to hire.

Requires Google Ads access. A 90-day search terms export is the single most useful first file.

Negative keyword strategy

Ready

This one does not need access. Virtual Coworker sells to employers and recruits talent, so employer campaigns need job-seeker language excluded before launch — not after the first invoice.

  • Job-seeker intent: jobs, hiring near me, apply, salary, work from home jobs
  • Career and training intent: how to become a VA, VA course
  • Free and DIY intent, unless deliberately targeted
  • Competitor terms handled as a separate deliberate decision, not by accident
Drafted, not applied. Becomes a real list once search terms confirm which of these are actually costing money.

Geo, language & schedule

Ready

Phase 1 settings I would apply on day one, and would defend:

  • United States only, set to presence rather than presence-or-interest
  • Excludes traffic from people merely searching about the US from elsewhere
  • Ad schedule aligned to when someone can actually answer the phone
  • AU stays in its own account and is not part of Phase 1

Budget & bidding

Blocked

Bidding strategy depends on the qualified-opportunity definition and on how much conversion history the account has. Both are open questions.

  • Start conservative while conversion data is thin
  • Move to automated bidding only when the data supports it
  • Optimise toward qualified opportunities, not raw form fills
Needs a decision from VC: monthly budget, and what counts as qualified. See Decisions.
