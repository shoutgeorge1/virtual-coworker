Performance Marketing · first 90 days

Executive Overview

How I would run paid acquisition as part of the Virtual Coworker team: audit first, build a clean Google Ads + landing-page system for US employers, hand tracked leads into Zoho, and judge success by completed qualified demos.

Approach

Leave what works. Build a cleaner paid layer beside it.

Strategy

As marketing manager I would not rip out the existing website. I would review historical Ads and lead data, prioritize the strongest US services and customers, ship dedicated paid landing pages, connect delivery into Zoho, then launch, measure, and improve — controlled first, scale only when quality supports it.

Own / drive

  • US employer Google Ads — audit, structure, management
  • Paid landing pages for ad traffic (separate from WordPress)
  • Tracking from click → lead → completed qualified demo
  • Zoho handoff with campaign attribution
  • Reporting on cost per completed qualified demo

Not the job

  • Rebuilding or repairing the WordPress sites
  • SEO / content ownership for organic
  • Replacing work the current team or agency already owns
  • PH talent recruiting as a paid priority in Phase 1

How we judge success

North-star KPI

Framework →

Cost per completed qualified demo — not cost per form fill.

Definitions still need team agreement: qualified lead, booked demo, completed demo, closed client. Until Ads and CRM history are visible, any CPL or demo-cost figures are planning ranges only — not promises.

Planning only

Conservative Google Search ranges

  • Cost per lead: ~$150–$300
  • Cost per completed qualified demo: ~$1,000–$2,000
  • With a first-month ad budget in the $10k–$20k range: roughly 35–100 leads and 5–15 completed qualified demos as a preliminary planning band

Industry reference points differ by channel; VC’s own history will replace these once access lands. Targets, not guarantees.
