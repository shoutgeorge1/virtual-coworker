How I’ll run paid

Paid often stalls when updates are infrequent and campaigns lean on a few broad keywords plus one landing page. I’ll run it as a steady operating rhythm — clear communication, and continuous testing.

  • Long-tail keywords — specific, high-intent phrases, not only expensive head terms. Cheaper clicks and clearer buyer intent when done well.
  • Many landing pages, tested — A/B and iterative variants by service, offer, and message. Not one page and hope.
  • Systematic review — search terms, waste, and quality against job orders every week; scale only what earns it.
  • Regular communication — short updates on what’s working, what’s cut, and what changes next. No black box.
