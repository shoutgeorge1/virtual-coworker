Lead Flow & Zoho

What should happen between someone submitting a paid enquiry and a salesperson picking it up. Everything on this page is a proposal until VC confirms how their Zoho actually works today.

Current state: unconfirmed. From the outside it is possible to see that Virtual Coworker uses Zoho Recruit for candidates. What is not visible — and must not be guessed — is which Zoho product holds employer enquiries, who administers it, or where website form submissions land today.

Proposed

How a paid lead should travel

Proposal
  1. SubmitEmployer completes the short form on a paid page
  2. CaptureLead saved with campaign, keyword, and page
  3. NotifyOwner alerted immediately, not on a nightly batch
  4. CreateRecord created in Zoho, tagged as paid
  5. ContactFirst human contact inside an agreed window
  6. MarkSales marks qualified or not, with a reason
  7. ReturnThat status flows back to bidding

Steps one to three sit inside the paid layer and are entirely mine to build. Steps four to seven touch VC's systems and people, which is why they need agreement rather than assumption.

Validation

Questions to answer before anything is wired

Needs validation

The system

  • Which Zoho product holds employer enquiries — CRM, Bigin, or something else?
  • Which edition? It determines what integration is even possible.
  • Is Zoho the source of truth, or does a spreadsheet or inbox do the real work?
  • Who administers it, and can they make field changes?

Today's flow

  • When someone submits a website form now, where does it go?
  • Email only, or an automatic CRM record?
  • Who is notified, and how fast is first contact in practice?
  • What happens to enquiries that arrive by phone or chat?

Data shape

  • Can we add a field for lead source, campaign, and keyword?
  • Is there already a paid-versus-organic tag, and is it trusted?
  • How are duplicates handled when someone enquires twice?
  • Are candidate and employer records properly separated?

Quality loop

  • Will sales mark each paid lead qualified or not?
  • What stage counts as a real opportunity?
  • Can that status be exported or sent back to Google Ads?
  • Who owns that habit, and what happens when they are on leave?

If integration takes time

A launch does not have to wait for perfect plumbing

Ready
  • Day one minimum: lead captured on the paid layer with full campaign data, emailed instantly to a named person, and logged where I can report on it.
  • Next: automatic record creation in Zoho with the same fields.
  • Then: qualified status flowing back to Google Ads so bidding optimises for buyers rather than form fills.

Each step is useful on its own. Nothing here requires a CRM migration, a new platform, or changes to how the existing website forms behave.

Deliberately not claimed: that the current CRM setup is broken, slow, or badly configured. None of that is visible from outside, and the honest answer is that the existing arrangement may already work well for the leads it receives today.
