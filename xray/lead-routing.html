Two steps

How Ads gets smarter over time

  1. Near-term
    Shallow Zoho qualify → Ads Sales (or ops) marks a decent qualified employer lead in Zoho. We try to send that signal into Google Ads with the click ID (GCLID). Goal: Ads learns “this kind of click was good” — without waiting for a closed deal.
  2. Later
    Value when money lands When there are enough real deals, upload or sync conversion values (job order / placement dollars). That’s when Maximize Conversions / value bidding can make sense. Not before.
