Keywords

We only bid on people trying to hire a VA — not people looking for a job. Volume in this niche is limited. That’s expected.

Honest volume picture. “Filipino / Philippines VA” employer searches are a small market — often low monthly searches per phrase. We won’t invent fake Keyword Planner numbers here. General “hire a VA” terms get more searches, but also more low-intent traffic and more competition. The plan: start tight and exact, protect quality, then carefully expand.

Simple rules

How we buy keywords

  • Exact match first — we know what we’re paying for
  • Phrase later — only after the search terms look clean
  • No broad match at the start
  • Low volume is OK — a few good employer leads beat a pile of job seekers
