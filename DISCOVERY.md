# Client discovery questions — paid External Funnel Audit

These are **paid engagement kickoff** questions. Not interview / job-seeker prompts. Ask after the public sample report is shared.

## Core (always)

1. **Who owns** GTM / GA4 / Google Ads / Meta / LinkedIn tags today — internal, freelancer, or agency — and what is the single source of truth?
2. **Where do form submissions land** (email, CRM, custom admin, Zapier/Make) for each public form ID we listed?
3. What is the **money conversion** (submit vs qualified meeting vs signed deal), and which of those fire in Ads / Meta / GA4 today?
4. Should **chat / phone / forms** create the same CRM record, or are they separate inboxes on purpose?
5. Are **buyer vs talent / support** funnels supposed to share analytics properties, or stay fully split?

## When regional sites exist

6. Why do regions run **different** GTM / GA4 / Ads IDs — intentional silos or drift?
7. Who is allowed to edit the **WordPress theme / plugins** vs GTM only?

## When funnel bleed is visible

8. Is a job-seeker / wrong-audience option on a buyer form **intentional**? If not, who removes it and by when?
9. Should careers CTAs appear on **every** buyer page, or only on dedicated paths?

## Out of scope reminders (say out loud)

- We will not request passwords in the discovery call.  
- Deeper CRM / Ads admin work is a **separate paid package** after public findings.  
- No form tests that create fake leads in their CRM.
