Findings below are evidence-based from public sources; anything behind login is out of scope.

4. CRM / lead routing clues

  • Buyer leads: Gravity Forms POST back to WordPress page URLs. Destination after submit unknown without submitting (email, webhook, Zapier, admin app — all possible).
  • Ops system: admin.virtualcoworker.com is a custom Rails app (login form /users/sign_in). Strong clue that fulfillment / client portal lives outside WordPress.
  • Talent leads: PH site routes to Zoho Recruit (jobs + candidate portal) — separate system from buyer forms.
  • Support: Zendesk on buyer site can become a second “inbox” for sales-ish chats if not routed cleanly.

External audit cannot confirm whether form → CRM is 1:1, duplicated, or manually forwarded. That’s a discovery item for a paid engagement.

5. Buyer vs candidate funnel contamination

Critical Consultation form includes a job-seeker option. On /contact-us/, Gravity Form fields include staffing hours (part-time / full-time) and an option labeled “I am searching for a job” next to buyer fields (company, country, hire intent). That mixes candidate traffic into the paid consult path — polluted CRM, wasted sales time, dirty conversion events.

High US buyer chrome constantly points to PH careers. Footer / global CTA: “Looking for career opportunities? Visit our Philippines careers site” → virtualcoworker.com.ph. Good intent (separate talent brand), but it means candidate intent is always one click away from every buyer page — and tracking stacks diverge (buyer: GTM+Meta+Ads; PH: essentially bare WP + Zoho).

High PH site still exposes buyer-ish URLs (pricing guide, services pages, link back to US/AU) while primary job of the domain is careers. Cross-links without hard funnel walls = analytics and SEO bleed both ways.

8. Agency dependence / weak tracking / chaos signs

  • Chaos Three regions, three tracking postures — US (GTM+GA4+Ads+Meta), AU (different GTM/GA4/Ads, no Meta on homepage), PH (almost no marketing tags). Hard to roll up “company” performance.
  • Chaos Meta pixel hardcoded on US while Google stack lives in GTM — classic “someone added a pixel in the theme / plugin” pattern. Ownership blur between WP admin and GTM editor.
  • Solid Custom Rails admin exists — not a pure brochure site; ops system is real.
  • Risk Dual contact forms (/contact-us/ vs /contact/) + chat + Zoho = multiple front doors without proof of unified attribution.
  • Risk Job-seeker option on buyer consult form is a smoking gun for lead-quality debt.
  • No CallRail / obvious call tracking — phone attribution likely weak if phone is a channel.

9. Client discovery questions (paid audit kickoff)

Framed for a paid engagement — not role/interview questions. Use these to scope a deeper audit + fix package.

1. Who owns GTM / GA4 / Google Ads / Meta — internal, freelancer, or agency — and is there one source of truth? Explains the split: hardcoded Meta vs GTM Google tags, and US vs AU container drift.
2. Where do Gravity Forms submissions land (email, CRM, Rails admin, Zapier), and is “I am searching for a job” still supposed to be on the consult form? Highest-ROI cleanup candidate; also maps lead routing for offline conversion work later.
3. How do you want buyer (.com / .com.au) vs talent (.ph + Zoho) analytics separated — separate properties, filters, or fully split stacks? Prevents candidate traffic from contaminating paid buyer conversion reporting.
4. Which conversion is the money event — form submit, qualified consult, signed client — and which of those fire in GTM / Google Ads / Meta today? GTM mentions generate_lead / purchase / Schedule; need to match to real sales stages.
5. Should Zendesk chat create sales leads, support tickets, or both — and are those joined to the same CRM record as the form? Second front door; often invisible in ROAS math.

Top findings (executive)

  1. US stack is mature-ish (WP + GTM + GA4 + Google Ads + Meta + Zendesk) but Meta is partially outside GTM.
  2. AU and US run different GTM/GA4/Ads IDs — regional silos, not one global container.
  3. PH careers site is a separate funnel (Zoho Recruit) with basically no marketing tag parity.
  4. Buyer consult form explicitly accepts job seekers — clearest public hygiene failure.
  5. Custom Rails admin suggests real ops backend; form→CRM glue is the unknown that a paid audit should map next.
