# Keyword strategy — Phase 1 Google Search pilot

Employer-side Search only. Quality over volume.

## Match types

| Priority | Match | Rule |
|----------|-------|------|
| 1 | **Exact** | Launch with exact only |
| 2 | Phrase | Add only after search-term quality is confirmed |
| 3 | Broad | **Not used** in the initial pilot |

Low volume is acceptable. Job-seeker traffic must be excluded aggressively (see `negative-keywords.md`).

## Primary clusters

### 1 — Philippines-specific VA hiring

Intent: businesses hiring or pricing Filipino / Philippines VA services.

Examples: `hire virtual assistant philippines`, `hire filipino virtual assistant`, `virtual assistant philippines cost`, `philippines virtual assistant company`, `filipino virtual assistant agency`

### 2 — General employer VA hiring

Intent: businesses looking for a VA company or hiring solution (position as Philippines-focused staffing even without “Philippines” in the query).

Examples: `virtual assistant for business`, `virtual assistant company`, `hiring a virtual assistant`, `need a virtual assistant`, `virtual assistant recruitment agencies`, `remote virtual assistants`

### 3 — Philippines bookkeeping outsourcing

Intent: outsource bookkeeping / hire remote PH bookkeeping talent.

Examples: `philippines bookkeeping outsourcing`, `virtual bookkeeper philippines`, `outsource bookkeeping philippines`

### 4 — General bookkeeping outsourcing

Intent: bookkeeping / accounting outsourcing (no PH term required).

Examples: `bookkeeping outsourcing`, `hire a bookkeeper`, `virtual bookkeeping companies`, `accounting virtual assistant`

## Phase 1 destination rule

- **Clusters 1 + 2** → existing market pages `/us` and `/au` (geo campaigns).
- **Clusters 3 + 4** → no dedicated page exists yet; **not built in this pass**. Documented as later-phase destinations unless Virtual Coworker prioritizes bookkeeping for Phase 1.

Recommended future paths (planning only): `/hire-virtual-assistant-philippines`, `/hire-virtual-assistant`, `/philippines-bookkeeping-outsourcing`.

## Do not block globally

Do **not** add account-level negatives for `hire` or `hiring` — those often signal strong employer intent.
